Background

Architect. Engineer.
Problem solver.

I'm Faraz — a Solution Architect and AI Engineer based in Western Sydney with over two decades of enterprise IT experience spanning loyalty platforms, cloud infrastructure, and applied AI.

I spent 18 years at Emirates Airlines, growing from software engineer to Solution Architect on the Skywards loyalty platform. I led deployments to Air Niugini, SriLankan Airlines, and Philippines Airlines, managed teams of ~20 engineers, and handled security architecture including OKTA SSO, BeyondTrust PAM, and ThreatMetrix.

Since relocating to Sydney in 2023, I've been contracting through HCLTech, working with Transport for NSW and Verizon's GovCloud Programme. I'm actively building at the intersection of cloud architecture and generative AI, with real hands-on POCs on AWS Bedrock and LangChain under my belt.

Outside of work: gym 3–4 days a week, high-protein diet, and a young family keeping life full and grounded.
